People switching from cable often build their new setup from a few pieces: something for live channels, something for films and series, maybe a sports app. So a common question before signing up is whether Apollo Group TV includes Netflix, or whether you still need a separate Netflix subscription.

The short answer is that Netflix isn't part of Apollo Group TV. Netflix is its own service, with its own app and its own account. The same goes for almost every major streaming brand. Once you know why, it's easy to plan a setup that covers what you watch without paying twice for the same thing.

This guide explains what Apollo Group TV includes, how it differs from Netflix, and how to run both comfortably on the same TV.

## The short answer

- **Apollo Group TV doesn't include Netflix.** A Netflix subscription is separate and is managed with Netflix directly.
- **Netflix original series and films are distributed by Netflix through its own service**, so they aren't part of Apollo Group TV.
- **You can use both on the same device.** Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV all run the Netflix app alongside Apollo Group TV.
- **They do different jobs.** Apollo Group TV is built around live channels plus an on-demand library; Netflix is an on-demand service built around its own catalog.

## What Apollo Group TV includes

Apollo Group TV, often shortened to Apollo TV, is an internet TV service. Every pack includes:

- **Live channels**, organized by category and region, with a channel guide.
- **An on-demand library** of films and series you can watch when you like while your pack is active.
- **HD, Full HD and 4K quality where available**, shown on each channel.
- **Apps and setup guides** for supported devices, listed on our [Apollo TV app page](/app).
- **Your account dashboard**, with your pack, payments and support history in one place.

Every pack has the same channels and library. Only the length and the price change, which you can compare on the [Plans page](/plans).

What Apollo Group TV doesn't include is access to other companies' services: no Netflix, no logins to other streaming apps, and no third-party accounts bundled in.

## Why streaming services stay separate

### Each service licenses and distributes its own content

Streaming companies negotiate rights to the films and series they show, and many produce their own. Netflix's originals are made or licensed for Netflix and distributed through Netflix. The same applies to other big streaming brands and their exclusive shows. A different service can't simply include them.

### Each service runs its own account and billing

Your Netflix plan, profiles, viewing history and payment details live in your Netflix account. Netflix explains its plans and pricing on its own [plans and pricing page](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/24926). Nothing about your Apollo Group TV pack changes your Netflix account, and the other way round.

### A warning about "bundles" you might see elsewhere

You may come across offers that claim to include Netflix accounts or "every streaming app" in one cheap package. Netflix accounts can only come from Netflix or from partners Netflix works with, such as some phone and internet providers. Anything else is a red flag. Our guide to the [official Apollo Group TV website](/blog/apollo-group-tv-official-website) explains how to recognize trustworthy providers and avoid lookalike offers.

## Running Apollo Group TV and Netflix on one device

You don't need separate hardware. Every device that runs Apollo Group TV can also run Netflix.

### On Fire TV and Android TV

1. Install the Apollo TV app with our [Fire TV and Android TV guide](/guides/fire-tv-android-tv). Our article on the [Apollo TV Downloader code](/blog/apollo-tv-downloader-code) explains the Downloader step.
2. Install Netflix from the Amazon Appstore on Fire TV, or Google Play on Android TV.
3. Sign in to each app with its own account.
4. Move both apps to the front of your home screen. On Fire TV, highlight an app, press the menu button (three lines) and choose **Move to front**.

On Fire TV, the remote's Netflix button opens Netflix directly, and the Home button takes you back to switch to Apollo TV.

### On Google TV

Google TV devices install Netflix from Google Play, and the Apollo TV app the same way as Android TV. Our guide to [Apollo TV on Google TV](/blog/apollo-tv-google-tv) covers the setup.

### On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Install Netflix from the App Store, and set up your player for Apollo Group TV with the login details from My Account, as shown in our [iPhone and iPad guide](/guides/iphone-ipad) and [Apple TV guide](/guides/apple-tv).

### On a Samsung or LG smart TV

These TVs usually have Netflix built in. To add Apollo Group TV to the same screen, our article on [Samsung and LG smart TVs](/blog/apollo-tv-samsung-lg-smart-tv) explains the options, such as a supported streaming stick in a spare HDMI port.

## Planning your internet for both

Netflix and Apollo Group TV can run at the same time on different screens, for example a match on the living-room TV while someone watches a series in the bedroom. Each stream uses bandwidth, and live TV is less forgiving than on-demand video because it can't buffer far ahead.

- Add up the streams your household watches at the same time, not just one.
- Allow extra headroom for live TV in the evening, when networks are busiest.
- Use a wired connection or a mesh node for the main TV if Wi-Fi is weak.

Our guide to the [internet speed you need for IPTV](/blog/internet-speed-for-iptv) shows how to add it up, and [Ethernet vs Wi-Fi on a Firestick](/blog/firestick-ethernet-vs-wifi) helps if the TV is far from the router.

### Does Netflix count toward my Apollo connection limit?

No. Your Apollo Group TV connection limit counts only streams playing on your Apollo login. Netflix has its own rules for how many screens can watch at once on your Netflix plan. Our article on [IPTV connection limits](/blog/iptv-connection-limit) explains how Apollo streams are counted.

## Building a setup without paying twice

A few practical ideas from households that have cut the cord:

- **Decide what each service is for.** Live sports and news, a broad library, specific originals: write down which service covers each, and drop any that only duplicate another.
- **Rotate on-demand services.** Some people keep one on-demand service at a time and switch when they've watched what they wanted. Live TV tends to stay constant.
- **Match the pack to your plans.** The 3-month Apollo pack is the shortest commitment; longer packs cost less per month. Compare them on the [Plans page](/plans).
- **Keep a list of renewal dates.** Monthly services renew until you cancel. Apollo Group TV's one-time packs don't renew automatically, so you choose when to buy the next one.

## Common misunderstandings

A few mix-ups come up again and again when people combine services:

- **"My Netflix login works in the Apollo app."** It doesn't. Each service has its own login. Your Apollo Group TV details are in [My Account](/dashboard/subscription), and your Netflix details stay with Netflix.
- **"Apollo can bill my Netflix plan."** It can't. Apollo Group TV only bills for Apollo packs. Netflix plans are paid to Netflix or to a partner Netflix works with.
- **"If I cancel Netflix, Apollo stops too."** No. The two accounts are completely independent.
- **"Netflix uses one of my Apollo connections."** No. Only streams on your Apollo login count toward its connection limit.
- **"The Netflix button on my remote opens Apollo."** No. It opens Netflix. Use the Home button to get back to your apps.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Apollo Group TV have Netflix?

No. Netflix is a separate service with its own app, account and billing. You can use it on the same devices as Apollo Group TV.

### Can I watch Netflix originals on Apollo Group TV?

No. Netflix original series and films are distributed by Netflix through its own service, so they aren't part of Apollo Group TV.

### Can I use Netflix and Apollo Group TV on the same Fire TV Stick?

Yes. Install both apps and switch between them from the home screen. Moving both to the front of the home screen makes switching quick.

### Is Apollo Group TV a replacement for Netflix?

They do different jobs. Apollo Group TV is built around live channels and an on-demand library, while Netflix focuses on its own on-demand catalog. Many households keep both.

### Does Apollo Group TV include other streaming apps?

No. Apollo Group TV doesn't bundle accounts for other services. Be wary of any offer that claims to include Netflix or other streaming accounts.

### Will using Netflix slow down Apollo Group TV?

Only if your connection can't handle both streams at the same time. Check your speed in the evening and allow headroom for live TV.

### Can I watch Apollo Group TV and Netflix at the same time on two TVs?

Yes, as long as your internet can carry both streams. Each service counts its own streams separately: the Apollo stream uses one of your Apollo connections, and the Netflix stream uses one of the screens on your Netflix plan.
