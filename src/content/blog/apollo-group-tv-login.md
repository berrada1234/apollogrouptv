Most sign-in problems with Apollo Group TV come from one mix-up: there are two different logins, and they don't work in each other's place. Your **account login** is the email and password you chose when you signed up. Your **player login** is a server URL, username and password made for IPTV player apps. Type the account password into a player app, or the player password into the website, and you'll get "invalid username or password" every time.

This guide shows where each login is used, how to sign in step by step, and how to fix the problems people hit most.

## The two Apollo Group TV logins

| | Account login | Player login |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Your email and the password you chose | Server URL, username and password |
| Where it's used | The website and the Apollo TV app | Third-party players, such as UHF on Apple TV and iPhone |
| Where to find it | You created it at sign-up | My Accounts → Player login, in your dashboard |
| How to reset it | "Forgot password?" on the Sign in page | Contact support |

### Account login: website and Apollo TV app

Your account is where your plan, invoices and support tickets live. You sign in with it on {{officialDomain}}, and the same email and password sign you in to the Apollo TV app on Fire TV, Android, Windows and Mac. One account covers the devices in your household, up to the device limit included with your pack. Our article on [IPTV connection limits](/blog/iptv-connection-limit) explains what happens when you go over that limit.

### Player login: third-party IPTV players

Some devices watch through a separate player app instead of the Apollo TV app. On Apple TV and iPhone, for example, you use the UHF player, as our [Apple TV guide](/guides/apple-tv) and [iPhone and iPad guide](/guides/iphone-ipad) show. These players need Xtream details: a server URL, a username and a password. You'll find them, along with an M3U link, under **My Accounts → Player login** once you're signed in. If the terms are new to you, read [Xtream Codes login explained](/blog/xtream-codes-login).

## How to log in to Apollo Group TV on the website

1. Open the [Sign in page](/sign-in). Check that the address bar shows **{{officialDomain}}** before you type anything.
2. Enter the email you used when you created your account.
3. Enter your password.
4. Tick **Keep me signed in on this device** if it's your own computer or phone. Leave it unticked on a shared device.
5. Select **Sign in**. Your dashboard opens, with your plan, invoices and My Accounts.

New here? [Create an account](/create-account) first. After you sign up, we email you a **6-digit code** to confirm your address. The code is valid for 10 minutes, and you can ask for a new one after 60 seconds. The [account setup guide](/guides/account-setup) walks through each screen.

### Where is the client portal?

"Client portal" is another name for your account dashboard. Sign in on {{officialDomain}} and it opens straight away. From there you can check your plan, pay or renew, copy your player login and contact support.

## How to sign in to the Apollo TV app

1. Install the app from the official link in the guide for your device. Start with [Fire TV and Android TV](/guides/fire-tv-android-tv) if you watch on a Firestick.
2. Open the app and choose sign in.
3. Enter the **same email and password** you use on the website.
4. Make sure your plan shows as **Active** in your dashboard. Watching needs an active pack.

If the app rejects a password that works on the website, update the app first. Old versions can fail to sign in. Our guide to [updating Apollo TV on a Firestick](/blog/update-apollo-tv-firestick) covers that.

## Forgot your password? Reset it in two minutes

1. On the [Sign in page](/sign-in), select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your account email and request the reset link.
3. Open the email and follow the link within **30 minutes**. After that it expires and you'll need a new one.
4. Choose a new password, confirm it and sign in.
5. Sign in again on each device with the new password.

Check your spam or promotions folder if the email doesn't arrive. If you no longer have access to the email on your account, [contact support](/contact) and we'll help you prove the account is yours.

A reset changes your **account** password only. Your player login stays the same. If you think someone else has your player details, ask support to issue new ones.

## Can't sign in to Apollo Group TV? Fixes in order

Work down this list. Most people are back in by step 3.

1. **Check which login you're using.** Website or Apollo TV app: account email and password. UHF or another player: the player login from My Accounts.
2. **Retype the password slowly.** Watch for an extra space at the end, Caps Lock, and autofill entering an old password.
3. **Reset the password** with "Forgot password?" if you're not sure what it is.
4. **Verify your email.** If you never entered the 6-digit code, the site sends you a new one when you try to sign in.
5. **Wait a minute after several tries.** Sign-in is rate-limited to protect accounts, so repeated attempts are paused briefly.
6. **Update or reinstall the app.** Install the latest version from the official link in your device's guide, then sign in again.
7. **Check whether the service has a problem.** If you're signed in but nothing plays, read [Is Apollo TV down?](/blog/is-apollo-tv-down) before changing anything else.

Still stuck? The [troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting) covers sign-in and playback errors, and our support team can check your account directly.

## Keep your Apollo Group TV account safe

- **Sign in only on {{officialDomain}}.** Several sites use names similar to ours. Our guide to [spotting fake Apollo TV sites](/blog/apollo-group-tv-official-website) shows how to check.
- **Use a password you don't use anywhere else.** CISA's advice on [strong passwords](https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/use-strong-passwords) recommends long, unique passwords kept in a password manager.
- **Turn on two-step sign-in** in your account's security settings, so a sign-in also needs a code from your phone. CISA explains why [multi-factor authentication](https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/turn-mfa) stops most account takeovers.
- **Treat login emails and messages with care.** We never ask for your password by email or chat. Learn the signs of [phishing](https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/recognize-and-report-phishing) and report anything suspicious to support.
- **Don't share your login outside your household.** Extra devices count toward your pack's limit, and a shared password can be changed by someone else.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Apollo TV login the same as the website login?

Yes. The Apollo TV app uses the same email and password as your account on {{officialDomain}}. Only third-party players use the separate player login.

### Where do I find my Apollo Group TV username and password for a player app?

Sign in on the website and open **My Accounts → Player login**. You'll see the server URL, username, password and M3U link to copy into the player.

### Why does it say my password is wrong when I'm sure it's right?

Usually because it's the other login. A player password won't open the website, and your account password won't work in a player app. Check for spaces and Caps Lock, then reset the password if it still fails.

### How long does the password reset link last?

30 minutes. Request a new link from the Sign in page if it has expired.

### Can I log in on more than one device?

Yes, up to the device limit included with your pack. Compare packs and device limits on our [plans page](/plans).

### I can't log in to renew my subscription. What should I do?

Use your account email and password, not the player login. Reset the password if needed, then follow the steps on the [renewal page](/apollo-group-tv-renewal).

## Conclusion

Apollo Group TV uses two logins. Your account email and password open the website, your dashboard and the Apollo TV app. The player login in My Accounts is only for third-party players. When sign-in fails, check which login you're using, reset the password if you're unsure, and update the app.

If none of the steps above work, [contact our support team](/contact) with the device you're using and the exact message you see.
