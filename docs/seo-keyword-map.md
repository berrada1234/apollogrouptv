# SEO keyword map: Apollo Group TV

**Metrics notice:** No SEO tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner) was available. **No search volumes, difficulty scores or rankings are stated anywhere in this document.** Intent and related questions come from manually sampling US web search results (2026-11-02) and the reference-site audit. Validate priorities with Search Console data after launch.

## SERP observations that shape the strategy

- Brand queries ("Apollo Group TV", "Apollo TV") return a crowded mix of lookalike domains, syndicated press releases and auto-generated "guide" pages making **conflicting claims** (different prices, channel counts and refund windows for the same name).
- The questions people search for are practical: how to install on Fire TV, Android or Smart TV; refunds; whether the service is legal; how to sign in.
- "Apollo TV" is ambiguous. It also refers to an unrelated media-player app, and "Apollo" on Reddit refers to an unrelated Reddit client.

**Differentiation strategy:** Be the **consistent, verifiable source**. One place for prices that match the plans database, policies with dates, device guides only for verified platforms, and plain answers to the legality and refund questions. Avoid the numeric hype the SERP is saturated with.

## Cannibalization controls

- **"Apollo Group TV app" (/app) vs "setup guide" (/guides).** /app is the hub and decision page. /guides holds the procedural steps. /app links to the guides instead of repeating steps.
- **"Apollo Group TV plans" (/plans) vs the homepage.** The homepage shows a short preview and points to /plans. Only /plans carries billing terms and the comparison table.
- **FAQ vs per-page FAQs.** Commercial pages carry 3–4 page-specific questions written separately. /faq is the canonical, complete set. No question appears word for word in two places.
- **"Is it legal" (/is-apollo-group-tv-legal) vs Terms and DMCA.** The explainer is educational. Terms and DMCA are contractual and procedural. They cross-link without duplicating text.
- **Old URLs** (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/support`, `/about`) redirect permanently (308) to the canonical versions.
