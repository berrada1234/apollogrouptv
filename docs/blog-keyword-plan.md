# Blog keyword plan (November 2026)

## Search data

No keyword research tool is connected to this project (no DataForSEO, Ahrefs or Semrush credentials in `.env`), so **search volume, competition and keyword difficulty were not measured**, and none are quoted here. Topics were chosen from the site audit below and from live Google results checked on 3 November 2026 to confirm each query has a clear informational intent and isn't answered by an existing page. Before investing in promotion, run the ten primary keywords through a keyword tool and record real numbers in this file.

## Internal linking map

- **Fire TV care** (1, 2) link to each other, to `/guides/fire-tv-android-tv` and `/guides/troubleshooting`, and to 3 and 4.
- **Devices** (3, 4) link to each other, to 1, 5 and 6, and to `/app` and the device guides.
- **Streaming tech** (5, 6) link to each other, to 4, and to `/plans` (connection limits) and `/faq`.
- **Player logins** (7, 8, 9) link to each other, to My Account (`/dashboard/subscription`) and to the iPhone, Apple TV and Windows guides where those players are used.
- **Trust** (10) links to `/about-us`, `/is-apollo-group-tv-legal`, `/payment-methods`, `/payment-guides/payment-troubleshooting` and `/contact`.
- Every article links to `/contact` or support once, and to `/plans` or `/` only where it's genuinely relevant.
